Samanvay Resident App Style and feature reference MODERNIST · AUG 2026

A resident-facing dashboard for a 60-flat housing society running automated electricity load balancing. Built as one interactive desktop mockup at a fixed 1280px on the Modernist design system: flat, architectural, Archivo throughout, zero corner radius, 2px rules between sections, everything flush left, nothing floating.

Type and spacing

Archivo at every level. Kickers are 10.5–11px at 800 weight with .14–.16em tracking, uppercase. Body is 15–16px, meta and captions 13px. Metrics step 28 / 32 / 40px at 800, with a single 72px hero figure. Section headings that need presence are 20px at 800 with a one-line subhead rather than a small-caps kicker.

Radius is 0 everywhere. Rules are 2px between major sections and 1px within them. Sibling groups are laid out with flex or grid and gap, never margins.

Frontend features

Five screens, one component

MY FLAT, SUGGESTIONS, SAVINGS, SOLAR and REQUESTS, reachable by click or the keyboard shortcuts Q W E R T, plus a settings modal that closes on backdrop click, the cross, or Escape. Tabs are boxed pills with the shortcut shown as a dimmed glyph and live count badges on the corner.

Live consent queue

Five suggestion types in a single-open accordion. Accepting or declining moves the item between arrays, auto-opens the next one, decrements the badge, and updates two running totals — what you save now, and what you would save if you took everything below. Declining opens a structured reason picker rather than just dismissing.

Tier cards

Four data-consent tiers as a 2×2 grid. Tier state is structural: an active tier gets a black 56px numeral block, black border and 3px black top rule; an inactive one goes grey. Tiers 2 and 3 are labelled buttons reading ON or TURN ON, not bare switches. Turning tier 2 off recomputes savings across the whole app.

Charts, all hand-built

No chart library. A day profile of 24 generation bars with usage as one continuous SVG polyline; a stacked savings bar wired to its legend by shared hover state; a contribution band strip with an over-hanging marker; a twelve-month bar chart; and a suggestion timeline strip.

Rules worth carrying to other projects

Money is always rupees, never kWh. Every automated action states its comfort cost before its saving. Every decline, toggle-off and pause is stated as free of penalty in the same place as the action. Estimates are labelled as estimates. Red appears as a field exactly once, on the emergency control — everywhere else the page is ink on ground.
